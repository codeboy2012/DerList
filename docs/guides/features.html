Features

A complete overview of what DerList can do.

📋 Wishlists

Create unlimited wishlists for any purpose — PC builds, gift lists, home decor, shopping plans.

  • Folders & Categories — Organize items into folders and categories within each wishlist
  • Star Priority (1–4) — Mark items by importance with a 4-level star rating
  • Share — Share wishlists publicly or with specific users via slug-based URLs
  • Archive — Archive completed wishlists to keep your workspace clean
  • Copy Wishlist — Duplicate an entire wishlist with configurable options
  • Copy to Clipboard — Export wishlist as formatted text for sharing anywhere

🛒 Product Editor

A full-featured product editor in a slide-out drawer with 8 dedicated sections:

  1. Info — Title, description, brand, URL, category
  2. Pricing — Current price, original price, deal info, currency
  3. Sellers — Multiple retailers with pricing per seller
  4. Images — Primary image and gallery management
  5. Wishlist — Priority, quantity, purchased status, notes
  6. AI — AI autofill, identification, and enrichment controls
  7. Specs — Technical specifications and attributes
  8. History — Price history chart and change log

🔍 Smart Import

Paste anything into DerList and it will figure out what you mean:

  • URLs — Product pages from any retailer
  • Product names — Plain text product names get identified and enriched
  • Shopping lists — Multi-line text with quantities and items
  • PCPartPicker builds — Full PC part lists imported with specs and pricing

🤖 AI Autofill

One-click enrichment that fills in missing product data:

  • Technical specifications
  • Sellers and pricing from multiple retailers
  • Product identifiers (SKU, MPN, ASIN, UPC)
  • Descriptions, images, and brand information

🧹 AI Organizer

Bulk cleanup operations powered by AI:

  • Rename — Standardize product titles across a wishlist
  • Categorize — Automatically assign items to categories
  • Deduplicate — Find and merge duplicate items

Works in two modes: analyze (preview changes) and apply (execute changes).

💰 Price Tracking

  • Price History — Visual chart of price changes over time
  • Multiple Sellers — Track the same product across different retailers
  • Price Lock — Record a target price for future comparison

💬 Shopping Assistant

An AI-powered chat interface for product recommendations. Ask questions like:

  • "What's the best GPU under $500?"
  • "Compare these two monitors"
  • "Suggest a keyboard for programming"

The assistant maintains conversation history and can add recommended products directly to your wishlists.

🔌 Provider System

DerList uses a pluggable provider architecture for AI, search, and pricing:

Provider Category Used For
OpenRouter AI Autofill, organizer, chat, parsing
OpenAI AI Autofill, organizer, chat, parsing
SerpAPI Search Product search, identification
Keepa Price Amazon price tracking and history

Providers support automatic failover — if one provider fails, DerList tries the next by priority order.
