DerList Documentation

The modern, open-source universal wishlist, shopping planner and PC builder. Free forever. Community driven.

Get Started API Reference

📋 Universal Wishlists

Create wishlists for anything — PC builds, home decor, gifts, shopping lists. Organize with folders and categories.

🤖 AI-Powered

AI Autofill enriches products with specs, images, and sellers. AI Organizer cleans and categorizes entire wishlists.

🔍 Smart Import

Paste URLs, product names, or entire shopping lists. DerList identifies products, finds prices, and enriches data automatically.

💰 Price Tracking

Track prices across multiple retailers. Get notified when prices drop. View price history charts.

🔒 Self-Hosted

Run on your own server. Your data stays with you. Invite-only access with full admin control.

🔌 Provider System

Connect AI, shopping search, and price tracking providers. Automatic failover between providers.
